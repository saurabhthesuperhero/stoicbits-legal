Privacy Policy

StoicBits, by Saikaizen Innovations. Effective 24 November 2026.

StoicBitsPrivacyTermsSupport

In short: no account, no ads, no selling of data. Your notebook, highlights and progress stay on your phone. We use Google Firebase to count app usage and to receive crash reports, and Google Play to handle subscriptions.

What stays on your device

Your notebook entries, morning and evening reflections, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading positions, practice history and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. They are not uploaded to any server we run. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted, unless your device's own Android backup restores them. You can export your notebook from the app at any time.

Narration

The voice that reads the texts runs on your device. No text or audio is sent anywhere to produce it.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC). They collect app interactions (for example which screens are opened and which features are used), crash and diagnostic data, the app version, device model and operating system, and an app-instance identifier. We use this only to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold. Google processes it under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The app receives from Google Play only whether your subscription or free trial is active.

Advertising

StoicBits shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs.

Children

StoicBits is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. To ask about data processed by Firebase for your app instance, or to request deletion, email us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date.
