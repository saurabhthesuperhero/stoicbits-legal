The app

StoicBits gives you the classic Stoic texts with an offline narration voice, daily practices, courses, remedies and a private notebook. It is for reflection and education. It is not medical or psychological treatment. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (for example 988 in the US).

Subscription

Full access requires a StoicBits Premium subscription, offered monthly or yearly through Google Play, at the price shown in the app before you buy. New subscribers may be offered a free trial; the length and the price after the trial are shown before you start. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, the subscription renews automatically and Google Play charges the renewal price. You can cancel anytime in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

Free taste

Without a subscription you can still read a small selection of passages, use the SOS breathing and crisis lines, and read and export your own notebook.

Texts and translations

The ancient texts are public domain. Some translations are public-domain editions (for example George Long, Aubrey Stewart), revised for modern readers; others are new StoicBits translations.
